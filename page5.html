You know how people make plans for places they want to visit? I think it's nice to have a few things to look forward to, even if we don't know exactly when they'll happen.

So here's a tiny invitation: maybe someday, we get to explore a new place, try something we've never tried before, laugh at something completely stupid, and collect a memory that belongs to that day alone.

And I think life is a little like that, too. We don't always know which turn comes next. We can plan, change direction, take a break when we're tired, and still find our way toward something good.

You don't need to have your whole future figured out right now, Lucky. One exam, one difficult day, or one uncertain chapter doesn't get to decide the whole story.

Some destinations are worth looking forward to.
Some journeys are worth taking one step at a time. 💙

Consider this a little wish for all the good things still ahead.
